# The French subjunctive

Verbs of desiring and wishing (volitive modality):
- Je veux que tu viennes. I want you to come.

Verbs of necessity or obligation (obligative modality):
- Il faut que tu partes. You must leave.

Verbs of doubt or uncertainty (speculative, dubitative?):
- Je doute qu’il ait raison. I doubt that he is right.

Adjectives of judgment or evaluation:
- Il est important que nous comprenions. It is important that we understand.

Verbs of emotional reaction:
- Je suis content que tu sois là. I am happy that you are here.

Purpose clauses:
- Je parle lentement pour que tu comprennes. I speak slowly so that you understand.

Concessives:
- Bien qu’il soit malade, il travaille. Although he is ill, he is working.

Relative clauses:
- Je cherche quelqu’un qui sache parler grec. I am looking for someone who can speak Greek.
