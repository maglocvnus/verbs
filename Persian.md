# Persian


Comrie (1976: 86 – *Aspect and voice*):
- *zan raft* ‘the woman went’, lit. woman.NOM went
- *pesar ketāb rā xarid* ‘the boy bought the book’, lit. boy.NOM book.ACC bought 

Comrie (1976):
- Persian has an invariable prefix *mi-* indicating imperfective aspect (p.88)
- The imperfective is *marked* in Persian (p. 114)
- (p. 121): In the Present Tense, apart from these few verbs, all Persian verbs occur only with the prefix *mi-*.


Present tense:
- *mi-xânam* ‘I read, I am reading’

Past tense:
- *mi-xândam* ‘I used to read, I was reading’
- *ketâb râ xândam* ‘I read the book’
- *ketâb râ mi-xândam* ‘I was reading the book, I used to read the book’
- raftam ‘I went’
- xordam ‘I ate’
- neveštam ‘I wrote’
- mi-raftam ‘I was writing, used to write’
- mi-xordam ‘I was eating, I used to eat’
- mi-neveštam ‘I was writing, I used to write’
