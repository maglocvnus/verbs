# λείπω

`λείπω` (leípō) ‘leave, abandon’ is an Ancient Greek (transitive) verb.


## Aorist indicative active



1st singular	ἔλιπον	élipon	I left
2nd singular	ἔλιπες	élipes	You left
3rd singular	ἔλιπε(ν)	élipe(n)	He/she/it left
2nd dual	ἐλίπετον	elípeton	You two left
3rd dual	ἐλιπέτην	elipétēn	Those two left
1st plural	ἐλίπομεν	elípomen	We left
2nd plural	ἐλίπετε	elípete	You (plural) left
3rd plural	ἔλιπον	élipon	They left
